Questions

What firms ask first

More on the fees and guarantee page, or ask Justin directly.

Do we pay anything before we hire?

No. There is no retainer and no fee to see profiles. The fee is due only if you hire someone we introduced, and the invoice goes out on their start date.

Can we keep posting the job and using other recruiters?

Yes. We ask for no exclusivity. Keep your posting live and work with anyone else you like.

Where do your candidates come from?

Attorneys and paralegals who have spoken with Jon in recent weeks and agreed to be shown anonymously. For a new seat we also search our wider network.

What if the hire leaves in the first months?

If they leave or are let go within 90 days of the start date, we search again and send replacements at no extra fee.

Do you fill paralegal and legal assistant seats too?

Yes. Attorneys, paralegals and legal assistants, for small and mid-size law firms. Other seats can be arranged.
