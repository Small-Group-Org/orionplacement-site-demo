Estate planning attorney pay

City detail is in each salary guide.

How much does an estate planning attorney make?

Across the cities we track, the median base is about $134k. It ranges from about $114k in San Antonio to $172k in New York.

What does an estate planning attorney do at a small firm?

Meets clients, designs the plan, drafts wills, trusts and powers of attorney, and usually runs trust administrations and probate matters too, often with one paralegal.

What raises an estate planning attorney's pay most?

A portable book of existing clients, often worth $15k to $30k on base. Estate tax returns and running administrations end to end come next.

Do estate planning attorneys get a bonus?

Often. Small firms pay a bonus on collections above a threshold, or a share of fees on clients the attorney brings in.
