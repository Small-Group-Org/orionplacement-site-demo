    Questions

    What candidates ask first

    Rather talk it through? Reach Jon directly.

    Will my current firm find out?

    No. We never contact your firm, and no employer sees your name or resume until you say yes to that specific role. Profiles we share before that show firm type and size only.

    Do I pay anything?

    No. The hiring firm pays Orion when you start. Your offer is not reduced to cover it.

    Are the pay bands real?

    Each band is the base range the firm agreed with us for that role. Bonus and benefits are on top and are shared on the first call.

    What if nothing listed fits me?

    Upload your resume anyway. Many roles are filled before they are posted, and Jon matches you to new ones as they open.

    Can I talk after work hours?

    Yes. Calls before or after work, or by email only, can be arranged. Say what suits you when you send your resume.
