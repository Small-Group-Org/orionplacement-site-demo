Before you post

Six things every posting needs

Postings with these get more replies from experienced people.

  • Put the base salary range in the posting.
  • Say whether the seat is on-site or hybrid, and which part of Houston.
  • Name the practice mix in plain words: wills and trusts, probate, administration.
  • State the licence you need. For attorneys, that is Texas.
  • Say who they report to and what paralegal support they get.
  • Say how and when you reply to applicants.

Posting in Houston

Where the people come from: law schools and bar groups in Houston.

Where should a Houston firm post an attorney job?

For licensed attorneys, the Houston Bar Association and State Bar of Texas career centres reach people who already practise in Texas. LinkedIn adds volume for mid-level seats.

Where should we post a paralegal or legal assistant job?

Indeed and LinkedIn bring the most applicants for staff seats. The Houston Paralegal Association job bank reaches experienced paralegals.

How soon will the first applicant arrive?

From the same day on Indeed to about 1–2 weeks on law school boards. Bar career centres sit in between, around 3–7 days.

Can we post and use a recruiter at the same time?

Yes. We ask for no exclusivity, and you pay us only if you hire someone we introduced.
