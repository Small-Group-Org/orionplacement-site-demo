QUESTIONS & ANSWERS · ZERO OBLIGATION

What firms ask first

More on the fees and guarantee page, or ask Justin directly.

What does it cost?

20% of the hire's first-year base salary, invoiced on their start date and due in 30 days. Nothing upfront and nothing if you do not hire. Terms for several hires can be arranged.

What if the hire leaves early?

If they leave or are let go within 90 days of starting, we search again and send replacements at no extra fee.

Do we have to work only with you?

No. Keep posting and keep your other recruiters. You owe a fee only for a hire we introduced.

How do you keep candidates anonymous?

Profiles show firm type and size, never names. The full redacted resume goes to you on request, and names follow when both sides agree to meet.

Which seats do you fill?

Attorneys, paralegals and legal assistants at small and mid-size law firms across the US. Other legal seats can be arranged; ask Justin.
