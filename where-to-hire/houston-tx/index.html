    Which law schools are in Houston?

    Three: the University of Houston Law Center, South Texas College of Law Houston and Thurgood Marshall School of Law at Texas Southern University. Each has a career office that takes postings from firms.

    Can a small firm recruit at Houston law schools?

    Yes. Career offices take postings from firms of any size, for clerks, summer associates and graduates.

    Where do experienced estate planning attorneys come from?

    Mostly from other small firms. In our sample, 46% of lateral hires came from other small firms.
