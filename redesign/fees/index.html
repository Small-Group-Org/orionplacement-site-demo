Replacement

The 90-day replacement, in plain English.

If a hire doesn't work out, you don't pay twice. Justin reopens the search and Jon sends new screened candidates until the seat is filled.

  • 90 calendar days

    Counted from the hire's first day at your firm.

  • A new search at no charge

    If the hire leaves or is let go in that time, we search again and send screened replacements. No second fee.

  • The same founders

    Justin runs the new search and Jon screens every replacement, just as the first time.

  • Can be customized

    Multi-seat searches and other arrangements can be agreed with Justin before the search starts.
