Clerkships and the junior pipeline

If you hire first-year associates, start with a summer or school-year clerkship. Thurgood Marshall opens summer clerkship postings from February, the University of Houston Law Center runs on-campus interviews in August, and South Texas College of Law takes clerk postings all year.

A clerk who has worked your files for a summer needs far less training as a first-year, and you will know their drafting before you make an offer. Post the clerkship with the same detail as an associate role: practice mix, hours and pay.
