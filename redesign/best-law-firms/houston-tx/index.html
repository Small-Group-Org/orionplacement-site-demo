The five tests

Every firm on the list passes all five. We judge firms on what makes associates stay, not on size or prestige.

  1. Test 1

    Size

    Small or mid-size; associates get client work early.

  2. Test 2

    Practice focus

    At least half the work in one practice area.

  3. Test 3

    Published pay

    A base range on every role it opens.

  4. Test 4

    Work arrangement

    On-site or hybrid stated up front.

  5. Test 5

    Public reviews

    4.5 or more on average, across employee and client reviews.
