Questions

Before you send the brief.

No posting written yet? Start from a hiring kit, or check pay first in the salary guide.

Is my job published anywhere?

No. We use your brief to search and screen. We don't post it on job boards unless you ask us to.

Can we keep our posting live and use other recruiters?

Yes. The agreement is non-exclusive, so your posts, referrals and other recruiters stay active.

What if our pay is below the market?

Justin will tell you on the first reply and show you the pay band for your city and level, so you can decide before the search starts.

What are the fees and terms?

23% of the hire's first-year base salary, only if you hire someone we introduced. Nothing upfront. Invoiced on the start date, due Net 15, with a 90-day replacement search at no charge. Terms can be customized for multi-seat searches. See the full terms.
