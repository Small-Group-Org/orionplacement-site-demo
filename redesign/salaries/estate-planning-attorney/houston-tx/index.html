What moves an offer

Three things move a Houston estate planning offer more than anything else. Plan for them before you set the band in your posting.

Portable book

+$15k–$30k

Even a small book of existing estate clients is the biggest single lever on base.

Trust administration

Up one band

Running administrations end to end, not only drafting, moves an offer up a level.

On-site seat

+$5k–$10k

Fully on-site Houston seats pay more than hybrid seats at the same level.

Questions

Estate planning attorney pay in Houston

What does an estate planning attorney earn in Houston?

The median base salary is $132k. Attorneys with 1 to 3 years generally earn $90k–$115k, those with 4 to 7 years $115k–$150k, and those with 8 or more years $150k–$195k, before any bonus or origination share.

Does a portable book of business change the base offer?

Yes. Even a modest book of existing clients is the strongest pay lever in Houston, typically adding $15k to $30k to base, often alongside an origination share of 25% to 33% of fees on matters the attorney brings in.

Do small firms pay less than large firms?

On base salary, Houston small firms usually sit below large-firm scales. They often ask for fewer billable hours (around 1,500 rather than 2,100) and add collections incentives, so total pay and hours can come out ahead.
