Privacy notice

How Orion handles your information.

This notice covers attorneys and other candidates who send us a resume, and law firms that hire through us. The short version: we collect what we need to make a good match, and no firm sees a candidate's name without the candidate's consent.

What we collect

Contact details, your resume and what you tell us about the roles or hires you want.

Why

To match candidates with law firms and to run each search.

Who sees it

Justin and Jon. No firm sees a candidate's name or resume until the candidate says yes.

Deletion

Email jon@orionplacement.com. We delete your information promptly on request.

Who we are

Orion Placement LLC ("Orion", "we") is a legal recruiting firm based in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. We place attorneys, paralegals and legal assistants with small and mid-size law firms. The firm is run by its two founders, Justin Sofman and Jon Missirlian.

What we collect

From candidates

  • Your name, personal email and, if you give it, your phone number.
  • Your resume, if you send one (PDF or Word), and anything in it: work history, education, bar admissions.
  • What you tell us on forms, by email or on calls: practice areas, target pay, notice period, the cities and firms you are interested in.
  • Notes Jon makes while screening you and discussing roles.

Please use a personal email address, never your current firm's.

From law firms

  • Contact details for the people we work with at the firm.
  • Details of the seat: practice focus, experience, pay range, location and work arrangement.
  • Interview feedback and offer details, so we can run the search and invoice correctly.

Why we use it

  • To match candidates with roles and firms with candidates.
  • To contact you about a search, a role or your application.
  • To prepare anonymous candidate profiles for firms.
  • To run our agreements with firms, including invoices and the 90-day replacement.
  • To meet legal obligations and keep records we are required to keep.

We do not sell personal information.

Who sees it

Inside Orion, your information is seen by Justin and Jon.

Candidates: before you agree, a firm sees only an anonymous profile with a reference code: practice mix, years in practice, bar state and target pay. Nobody at a firm sees your name, contact details, current firm or resume without your consent, and you give that consent one firm at a time. We never contact your current firm.

Firms: we share a firm's name and the details of a seat with a candidate when it helps them decide whether to interview. A firm can ask us to keep its name confidential until a later stage.

We also use a small number of service providers, such as email and file storage, to run the business. They handle information only on our instructions. Orion can list them here on request. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep a candidate's file while it is useful for matching them with roles, and a firm's records for as long as we work together and as needed for our agreements and legal obligations. Ask us to delete your information and we will do so promptly. The exact retention periods can be set out by Orion in the final version of this notice.

Your choices and deletion

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct it,
  • stop contacting you about roles, or
  • delete it.

Email jon@orionplacement.com from the address we have on file. We act on deletion requests promptly and confirm by email when it is done. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under state law; tell us and we will help.

This website

Forms on this site collect only what you type or attach. The salary check runs in your browser, and the numbers you enter are not sent to us. Orion can list any analytics or cookies used on this site here.

Security

We limit access to the founders, use reputable providers and keep candidate files under anonymous codes when sharing with firms. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.
