    Reading the band

    What moves your number inside the band.

    Two attorneys with the same years can sit $30k apart. These are the usual reasons.

    Practice mix

    Work that a firm bills at higher rates, like tax-heavy planning or commercial trials, moves you toward the top.

    Running matters alone

    Firms pay more for attorneys who run files without a partner in the room and supervise staff.

    Book of business

    Even a small book or steady referrals often shows up as origination bonus on top of base.
