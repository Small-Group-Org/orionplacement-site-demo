Questions

What attorneys ask first.

Something else? Email jon@orionplacement.com from a personal address.

Will my current firm find out?

No. Orion never contacts your firm, and no firm sees your name or resume until you say yes to that specific firm. Before that, a firm sees only an anonymous profile: practice mix, years of experience, bar standing and target pay.

Do I pay anything?

No. It is free for candidates, always. The hiring firm pays Orion's fee, and only if it hires you.

Are the posted salary ranges real?

Yes. Each range is the base pay the firm has confirmed for that seat. Jon also tells you what he knows about bonus and billable expectations on the first call.

I'm not in Texas. Should I still send my resume?

Yes. The roles open today are in Texas, and Orion places attorneys in other states too. Send your resume anyway, and Jon will tell you what is open for your practice and state.

Nothing listed fits my practice. Now what?

Send your resume anyway. Many seats are filled before they are posted, and Jon matches you as new searches open.

Can we talk outside work hours?

Yes. Tell Jon the times that suit you and the call is set around your schedule.

How do I get my file deleted?

Email jon@orionplacement.com and ask. Your file is deleted promptly on request. The privacy notice has the details.
