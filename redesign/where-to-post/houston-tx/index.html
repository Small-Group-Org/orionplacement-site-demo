Six things every legal job posting needs

Postings that answer a candidate's first questions get fewer, better applicants. Wherever you post, include these.

  1. The base pay band. A range filters out mismatched applicants and shows the firm is straightforward. Use the Houston salary guide to set it.
  2. The work arrangement. On-site or hybrid days, and the office location (Downtown, Galleria and so on).
  3. The practice mix. For example: 60% estate planning, 40% probate and trust administration.
  4. Bar requirement. Texas license in good standing, or the reciprocity you will accept.
  5. The billable target. A realistic annual number, such as 1,400 to 1,750 hours.
  6. Confidentiality. Say who reads applications and that resumes are never circulated.
