The Houston legal market for small firms

Houston's small and mid-size firms hire across estate planning, litigation, insurance defense, personal injury, family law, real estate and employment. The firms we recruit for sit Downtown, in the Galleria and Uptown, Greenway Plaza, Memorial, Midtown, the Energy Corridor, Katy and The Woodlands.

Most lateral hires into these firms come from other small firms. Candidates moving from large firms usually want fewer billable hours and earlier client work, and will often trade some base for it. Read where Houston hires come from for the full breakdown.
