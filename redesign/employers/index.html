Questions

What firms ask first.

Free hiring resources: salary guides, hiring kits and where to post a job.

Do we pay anything before we hire?

No. The fee is 23% of the hire's first-year base salary, and only if you hire someone we introduced. The invoice is sent on the start date and due in 15 days.

Can we keep posting the job and using other recruiters?

Yes. The agreement is non-exclusive, so you can keep your job posts, referrals and other recruiters.

Where do your candidates come from?

Jon searches our own network, including attorneys who are not on job boards, and screens each one for practice mix, bar standing, pay and notice before you see a profile.

What if the hire leaves in the first months?

If they leave within 90 days of the start date, we run a replacement search at no charge.

Do you fill paralegal and legal assistant seats too?

Yes. We place attorneys, paralegals and legal assistants at small and mid-size law firms, with a focus on Texas and searches in any US state. Terms can be customized for multi-seat searches.
