    How a dossier is made

    You see the attorney before you see the name.

    Most agencies forward a stack of résumés. We send one page per candidate, screened by a founder, with the name and current firm protected until both sides want to talk.

    01 · Read

    Every résumé is read by a founder.

    Jon reads every application and speaks with every candidate himself. No junior recruiters, no keyword filters.

    02 · Screen

    Screened against your seat.

    Bar admission, practice mix, court or deal experience, billable expectations and pay band are checked before you see anyone.

    03 · Protect

    Name and firm stay confidential.

    You get the facts that matter for the hire. The candidate's identity is protected, and so is your search.

    04 · Meet

    You choose who to meet.

    Request an introduction and we share the full profile once the candidate agrees. You pay only if you hire.

    Questions

    What firms ask first.

    Have a specific search in mind? Book 15 minutes with Justin.

    What does it cost?

    23% of the hire's first-year base salary, and only if you hire someone we introduced. Nothing upfront. The invoice is sent on the start date and due in 15 days. Terms can be customized for multi-seat searches.

    What if the hire leaves early?

    If they leave within 90 days, we run a replacement search at no extra charge.

    Do we have to work only with you?

    No. The agreement is non-exclusive, so you can keep your other recruiters and your own job posts.

    How do you keep candidates anonymous?

    You see a one-page dossier with the practice mix, experience, bar admission and pay band. The name and current firm are shared only after the candidate agrees to meet you.

    Where do you recruit?

    Law firms across the US. We fill attorney, paralegal and legal assistant seats at small and mid-size firms, and other legal roles can be arranged; ask Justin.
