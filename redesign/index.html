    Questions

    What firms ask first.

    Why did you email me?

    We write to small and mid-size firms whose practice areas match attorneys Jon is screening right now. If the timing is wrong, reply "no thanks" and we won't email you again. Orion emails only ever come from @orionplacement.com.

    What does it cost?

    23% of the hire's first-year base salary, and only if you hire someone we introduced. Nothing upfront. The invoice is sent on the start date and due in 15 days. Terms can be customized for multi-seat searches.

    What if the hire leaves early?

    If they leave within 90 days, we run a replacement search at no extra charge.

    Do we have to work only with you?

    No. The agreement is non-exclusive, so you can keep your other recruiters and your own job posts.

    How do you keep candidates anonymous?

    You see a one-page profile with the practice mix, experience, bar admission and pay band. The name and current firm are shared only after the candidate agrees to meet you.

    Where do you recruit?

    Law firms across the US. We fill attorney, paralegal and legal assistant seats at small and mid-size firms, and other legal roles can be arranged.

    Talk to Justin, not a call center.

    Fifteen minutes on the seat you need to fill. If we're not the right fit, he'll tell you. If we are, you'll see screened profiles in about a week.
