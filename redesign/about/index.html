About Orion Placement

Two founders who place attorneys at small and mid-size law firms.

Is this email from Orion?

Orion emails only come from @orionplacement.com.

If a message about Orion uses any other domain, forward it to justin@orionplacement.com before you reply.

A real Orion email never:

  • asks a candidate for any fee, at any stage
  • asks a firm for money upfront or a wire transfer
  • shares a candidate's name before the candidate says yes
  • comes from a Gmail, Yahoo or other webmail address

Talk to the person who runs your search.
