Estate planning pay in Houston

What does an estate planning attorney earn in Houston?

The median base is $132k. Attorneys with 1 to 3 years usually earn $90k to $115k, 4 to 7 years $115k to $150k, and 8 years or more $150k to $195k.

Does a portable book change the offer?

Yes. A small book of existing estate clients is the biggest single lever, often worth $15k to $30k on base, and can be paired with an origination bonus.

Do small firms pay less than large firms?

At small and mid-size firms, base is close to the bands above. Many add a bonus on collections or a fee split that larger firms do not offer.
