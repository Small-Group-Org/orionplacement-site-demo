Questions firms ask

Before you start

Do we have to stop using our other recruiters?

No. The agreement is non-exclusive. Keep your posting, referrals and other recruiters. You pay Orion only for a candidate Orion introduced who starts with you.

What exactly do we pay, and when?

Nothing up front. The fee is a percentage of the hire’s first-year base salary, due 15 days after they start. If you do not hire, you owe nothing. Ask about a reduced rate for a second hire.

What if the hire leaves early?

If they leave or you let them go within 90 days of starting, we run a replacement search at no extra charge.

Are these attorneys actively looking?

They are open to a move and have told us their pay expectation and when they could start. Each profile says when we last spoke to them.

Will our team or the market know we are hiring?

No public job post is made. Candidates see the role without your firm’s name until you agree to meet them.

Which cities and practice areas?

Firms across the US, most often in estate planning, litigation, real estate, personal injury and insurance defense. Tell us the seat, and if it is outside our network we will say so on the first call.

How do we know an email is really from Orion?

Orion emails come only from orionplacement.com, signed by Jon or Justin. If a message uses another domain, forward it to us before you reply.
